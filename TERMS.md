## What FixSnap is

FixSnap is a free assistant that suggests likely causes, step-by-step repair guides, and rough cost estimates for common home problems, based on photos and descriptions you provide.

## Important safety limits

- FixSnap's diagnosis is a **suggestion, not a professional inspection**. Confidence scores are estimates.
- For anything involving **gas, sparking or burning electrical faults, or structural/load-bearing damage**, FixSnap will advise you to hire a licensed professional — follow that advice.
- In an emergency (active flooding, gas smell, fire risk), contact a professional or your local emergency number first.
- Cost figures are US ballpark ranges for parts or typical professional pricing, **not quotes**.
